**RecOmni** is an all-in-one local media studio designed for creators, mobile gamers, and power users. Record your screen with a movable facecam, convert batches of videos or extract audio, and compress large files—all processed directly on your device with **no watermarks, no time limits, and no subscriptions**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎥 Screen Recording & Screenshots
* **High-Resolution & High-FPS Capture:** Record from `480p`, `720p`, and `1080p` up to `4K` & `8K` at `24`, `30`, `60`, or `120 FPS` using `H.264`, `H.265 (HEVC)`, `AV1`, or `APV`.
* **Flexible Audio Mixing:** Record **Mute**, **Internal Audio**, **Microphone**, or **Dual Audio (Internal + Mic)** simultaneously with independent microphone boost (`100%–300%`) and system volume controls.
* **Clean Floating Controls:**
  * **Radial Floating Dial:** Quick `REC` and `SNAP` shortcuts that automatically hide during active recording.
  * **Movable Facecam:** Draggable, 3-size front or back camera overlay baked directly into your video.
  * **9-Zone Invisible Stop Hotspot:** Tap an invisible corner, edge, or center spot to stop recording discreetly.
* **Instant Screenshots:** Save screenshots in **JPEG**, **PNG**, or **WEBP** directly to `Pictures/RecOmni`—even mid-recording.

### 🔄 Batch Video & Audio Converter
* **Multi-Format Export:** Batch convert videos to **MP4** or **MOV**, or extract soundtracks to **M4A (AAC)**.
* **Global or Per-Video Settings:** Fine-tune resolution, frame rate, codec (`H.264`, `H.265`, `AV1`, `APV`), and bitrate individually per clip or across the entire queue (with folder import supported).
* **Quality-Preserving Codec Scaling:** Automatically adjusts target bitrates when converting from higher-efficiency codecs (such as `H.265` or `AV1`) to broader compatibility codecs (such as `H.264`) to prevent quality loss.
* **Lossless Audio Passthrough:** Compatible AAC audio tracks are preserved without re-encoding whenever possible.

### 📉 Adaptive Video Compressor
* **Smart Size Reduction:** Choose `Low`, `Medium`, `Auto`, `High`, or a `Custom` bitrate to shrink massive videos for sharing or freeing up storage.
* **Quality Floor Safeguard:** Automatically checks video specifications to warn you before compressing an already-optimal file, preventing unnecessary pixelation.

### 📊 Before & After Comparison
* **Side-by-Side Stats:** Compare original vs. processed file size (`%` change), duration, format, codec, color space, resolution, frame rate, and audio stats.
* **Built-In Player & Cleanup:** Preview original or exported media right in the app and delete unwanted files in one tap.

### 🌍 Languages & Interface
* Adaptive Light/Dark themes with responsive layout for phones and tablets.
* Translated into **13 languages**: English, Afrikaans, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Access
* **100% On-Device Processing:** RecOmni uses no external servers and never uploads your videos, audio, or screenshots. Files save directly to `Movies/RecOmni`, `Pictures/RecOmni`, and `Music/RecOmni`.
* **No Subscriptions:** PRO features (`4K/8K`, `60/120 FPS`, `AV1/APV`, `Dual Audio`, `Custom Bitrate`, `Invisible Stop Button`, unlimited queue & folder import) are available as a **one-time unlock**—or via **stackable PRO Passes** in the free version (a pass is only used when a recording or export actually uses a PRO setting).

> **Hardware Note:** Availability of `4K/8K`, `120 FPS`, HDR Facecam, and `AV1/APV` depends on your individual device's hardware support.
