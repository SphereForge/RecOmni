# Privacy Policy for RecOmni

**Last Updated:** 28 August, 2026  
---

## 1. Overview & Local Data Processing

RecOmni is an all-in-one screen recording, facecam capture, video conversion, and video compression tool. 

**All video recordings, audio tracks, converted files, and photos are processed 100% locally on your device.** SphereForge does not host external media storage servers and does not access, view, or upload your personal media files.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Used by RecOmni

To deliver its core features, RecOmni requests the following device permissions:

* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Used strictly when you enable the floating facecam or camera overlay to capture video/photos.
* **Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used to capture microphone audio and mix dual audio (internal sound + mic) during recording sessions.
* **Storage / Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`):** Used solely to read videos you select for compression or conversion and save exported results to your device gallery (`Movies/RecOmni` and `Pictures/RecOmni`).
* **Screen Recording (`MediaProjection` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION`):** Used exclusively to record your screen when you initiate a recording.
* **Overlay (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`):** Used to display floating controls and the facecam window over other applications.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to display recording status, foreground service controls, and stop actions.

## 3. Third-Party Advertising & Mediation SDKs

The free version of RecOmni integrates third-party advertising SDKs to serve interstitial, rewarded, and native ads. These partners may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and telemetry data for advertising and fraud prevention:

* **Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP):** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Unity Ads:** [Unity Technologies Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
* **Unity LevelPlay (ironSource):** [ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy](https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/)

*Note: If you purchase the Pro version of RecOmni, all third-party advertising SDKs and data collection for advertising purposes are completely disabled.*

## 4. User Consent & Privacy Rights (GDPR & US State Regulations)

RecOmni complies with regional data privacy frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), UK GDPR, and applicable United States state privacy regulations.

* We utilize the certified **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to gather consent in regions where legally required.
* Users may review, revoke, or modify their consent preferences at any time through the in-app settings.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

RecOmni is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

---

## 6. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Because all video processing and exports occur locally on your device hardware using Android's native media pipelines, your personal recordings remain in your direct custody.
